# Result: a system that ends the day at +X before -X more than 60% of the time (2026-10-13)
PREREG_BARRIER.md, no amendments. Placebo worlds 101 (smoke run, tag `smoke`), 102, 103: 0 of 8 cells each (max VAL mean R +0.018, max VAL hit rate 53%) -> gate passed.
Real run: **0 of 8 cells pass**; best VAL mean R +0.015 per trade (crypto k=2, 190 trades; the bar was +0.20, i.e. a 60% hit rate with symmetric barriers). The highest hit rate, 58% of decided trades, is idx k=2 with 5,659 trades, but its mean R is -0.016 because the timeouts (neither barrier within 8 hours) lose on average; a hit rate among decided trades is not the quantity that pays. Best excess z 1.40 (crypto k=2, p = 0.089) against a placebo ceiling of 0.83 in the worlds run: not significant, not even before the Bonferroni 0.05/8. The spread alone makes a symmetric bracket start near 47-49%; the model finds no condition that lifts it to 60%.
Reading: nothing in MT5-native bars (price, spread, realised variance, tick volume, time) selects bracket entries with a 60% hit rate on 2025 data, on four asset classes, at 1 and 2 hourly sigmas. Ledgers: data\si_ledgers\BARRIER_real.csv, BARRIER_pl102/103.csv, BARRIER_smoke.csv.

# v2: the position is held until it hits +X or -X (PREREG_BARRIER2.md, 2026-10-13)
the trader: "o sistema deve continuar a operar até atingir um dos limites". Window 480 bars, rollover hours skipped for barrier checks, widths k in {2, 4} hourly sigmas, 8 cells. Calibration: placebo worlds 111, 112, 113 -> 0 of 8 each (max VAL mean R +0.046 / +0.199 / +0.157, max hit 52 / 60 / 60%, max z 1.65 / 2.29 / 1.66): gate passed, but note the placebo cells reach 60% hit with tiny n, so a hit rate alone means nothing here.
Real run: **0 of 8 cells pass**. One cell is above the placebo noise: fx k=4, VAL n=4,451, hit 56%, mean R **+0.104** (bar +0.20), excess z 2.94, p = 0.002 (Bonferroni 0.0063), median hold 81 h. It fails the pre-registered effect-size floor, so it is NOT a pass. All other cells: z between -1.5 and +1.4.
Audit of that cell (audit_fx4.py): 4,447 of 4,451 trades are LONGS, concentrated in the JPY crosses and the commodity currencies (GBPCAD +0.45 R, AUDJPY +0.40, NZDJPY +0.34, GBPJPY +0.29, CHFJPY +0.29, EURJPY +0.26, AUDUSD +0.24; the losers are USDCHF, CADCHF, USDJPY, GBPAUD); it is spread over 28 pairs (3 busiest = 14% of trades) and both halves of 2025 (+0.094 / +0.115). So it is one directional regime of 2025 (a yen-and-risk-currency trend), not a timing rule; on window B the same model made only +0.034 R. Swap is not modelled (long high-yield against JPY earns it, the reverse side pays it).
Reading: no system with a >60% chance of ending at +X before -X. The closest thing, long-only trend in the high-yield crosses, is 56% with +0.10 R, depends on 2025, and was not stable between windows. The sealed 2026 hold was not opened for it.
