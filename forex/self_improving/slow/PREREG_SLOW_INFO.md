# Pre-registration: new information at horizons of days to weeks (written 2026-10-13, before any code of it ran)

the trader: "try the new information" (after the frequent-trading family closed with 0/48). The information that the earlier families lacked and that is obtainable: **weekly positioning (CFTC Commitments of Traders)**, **macro-release surprise proxies from first-release vintages (ALFRED)**, and **lagged cross-asset daily returns**. Positioning and releases are slow information, so the family works at horizons where costs are small: 1, 5 and 20 trading days (a 1-4 bp spread against a 40-130 bp daily move; break-even information coefficient about 0.01-0.04).

## Universe, clock, windows
All 46 MT5 broker symbols in the 4 classes (idx 6, fx 28, cmd 4, crypto 8). One decision per weekday at the close of the 15-minute bar ending 12:00 server time (never in the rollover hours 23 and 0, and never within a day of a rollover by construction); entry at the ask (long) / bid (short) of the next bar's open; exit at the bid (long) / ask (short) close of the bar ending 12:00 server time H trading days later, H in {1, 5, 20}; real bid and ask, plus the flat conservative carry of the earlier tests (0.5 / 0.7 / 1.0 / 3.0 bp a calendar day by class). Windows: A 2022-01-01..2023-06-30 (training), B 2023-07-01..2024-12-31 (early stopping and threshold), VAL 2025 (one look per cell), 2026 sealed and never loaded (the same HOLD as the slow families).

## Information (all known at the decision; thresholds learned on A only)
- **Positioning (COT legacy futures-and-options combined, 2020-2026 files on disk, as-of Tuesday, released Friday 15:30 New York, so a report is usable only from the Friday evening after its as-of date: at a Monday 12:00 decision the latest report is 6 days old):** non-commercial net position over open interest and commercial net position over open interest, their percentile over the trailing 156 weeks, and their 1-week and 4-week changes, for CME EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, NZD futures, ICE dollar index, COMEX gold and silver, NYMEX/ICE WTI, S&P 500 (E-mini), Nasdaq-100, DJIA, Nikkei, CME bitcoin and ether. A currency pair uses the base minus the quote positioning (the USD side contributes the dollar index); crosses the difference of the two; markets without a contract are missing.
- **Macro surprises (ALFRED first-release vintages, available at the release time 08:30 New York):** first-print non-farm payroll change minus the mean of the three previous changes (in each vintage), first-print unemployment rate change, first-print CPI month-on-month change minus the mean of the previous three, the latest FOMC target-rate change (FRED DFEDTARU), each with the number of days since its release. No consensus forecast exists in the data: these are naive surprises against a trailing mean, a known weak proxy, written as such.
- Macro regime with the two-business-day lag (VIX rank and change, curve, dollar, VIX9D/VIX, SKEW rank), days to / since each scheduled release, lagged cross-asset daily returns (1 and 5 days, previous US close: ES, NQ, DIA, EWG, EWJ, ZN, TLT, 6E, 6J, GC, SI, CL, HG, NG, BTC), and price features at the decision (returns over 1, 5, 20, 60 days over the daily volatility, distance to 20 / 60 / 120-day mean, daily RSI, 60-day range position, 5-day over 60-day volatility). About 70 numbers.

## Models and tests (same discipline as PREREG_ML.md)
XGBoost per cell (class x H = 12 cells, same fixed settings, min child weight scaled to the smaller sample), target = the gross mid-to-mid return over the volatility of the horizon, winsorised at +/-5; trade the side of the prediction when |prediction| >= tau, tau = the quantile of |prediction| on B (50, 70, 80, 90, 95%) with the best net mean and at least 100 trades; trades on a grid of spacing H trading days per market (non-overlapping). A cell passes only if on VAL: >= 100 trades, >= 20 weekly clusters, net mean >= +3 bp, and the gross excess over random timing (same market, side, horizon, grid) has cluster-robust weekly Student-t p < 0.05 / 12. Any pass goes to the MT5 broker environment and to a per-feature audit (does the signal come from the new information or from price features; ablation without COT and surprises) before anything else.

## Calibration (before the real run)
Three never-seen placebo worlds (seeds 71, 72, 73): the identical pipeline with all labels of a row circularly shifted by a common fraction of the timeline (features and all real costs kept). Gate: 0 passing cells in every world; the noise ceiling (max VAL net, max VAL excess z) is reported next to the real numbers.

## Expectations written now
COT positioning is a slow, crowded, widely published signal and the macro surprise proxies are crude; the sample is small (about 4 yearly independent 20-day observations per market). The most likely outcome is no passing cell. If a cell passes, the ablation decides whether the new information, and not price, carries it.

Calibration gate: PASSED. Placebo worlds 71, 72, 73: 0 of 12 cells pass in every world; noise ceiling over the 36 cells: max VAL excess z 2.22 (the Bonferroni bar is p < 0.0042, about z 2.7 at 50 clusters). The information coefficient is NOT a reliable statistic here (20-day targets overlap; IC up to +0.35 in a placebo world with a few thousand overlapping rows), so the decision rests on the clustered tests and the net floor only. Real run follows with the same code.
