Hypothesis (frozen): after a strong tier-1 USD consensus surprise (|S| >= 1, sign by the calendar impact flag, scaled by the causal sd of the event's previous surprises; tier-1 = NFP, unemployment, earnings, CPI, retail sales, GDP, jobless claims, PMIs, FOMC) the basket of USD pairs (same mapping as tick_event_lab) is faded: enter AGAINST the surprise direction at +60 s (300 ms latency, market order at the quote, spread paid), exit at +300 s.
Test data: events with release time >= 2026-08-16 (ticks and the calendar must be re-exported read-only from the MT5 broker terminal; nothing of them has been looked at). Run when at least 25 such events exist (expected December 2026).
Pass: n >= 25, mean net > +0.5 bp, t >= 2.0 against zero, AND beating the same trade on the placebo calendar (event times + 7 days) by t >= 1.65. No parameter (delay 60 s, hold 240 s, |S| >= 1, tier-1 only) may change after seeing the data; one look.
Meaning of a pass: a possible per-event edge of about 1 bp net, which at any size we can trade (a few lots of gold, a few events a month) is worth a few dollars an event: it does NOT make the test faster; it only counts as the first information-based signal. Meaning of a fail: this line is closed for good.
