## 1. Clock: M1 information, 15-minute decisions
All M1 bid/ask OHLC bars (2021-12..2026-10, 46 symbols) are used. Each M1 bar is aggregated into the 15-minute bar it belongs to (server-time quarters), keeping bid and ask OHLC AND M1-derived statistics of the bar: realised 1-minute variance, Parkinson variance from the M1 ranges, number of moving minutes, share of up minutes, mean / max / last-minute spread, the return of the last 5 minutes, the largest 1-minute range. The agent decides at every 15-minute bar close, enters at the ask (long) / bid (short) of the NEXT bar's open and exits at a bar close H bars later (H in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 96, 192} bars = 15 min .. 2 days), with real bid and ask at both ends. Why not every minute: 15x more rows that are almost the same observation, overlapping holds, and a 1-2 bp spread that swallows any 1-minute move; the minute information enters through the features, not the decision frequency. This is a design choice, stated so nobody reads 'decision every 15 minutes' as 'ignores minute data'.

## 2. Information (all causal; thresholds from window A only; about 75 features)
Price at several scales (returns normalised by the bar volatility over 1 to 192 bars, RSI on 15m and 60m bars, distance to moving averages over 4 h / 1 d / 5 d, range position, volatility ratios), the M1-derived micro-structure above, time (hour, quarter-hour, weekday), previous-day high / low / close and today's high / low so far (distance in daily-volatility units; the day is the server date, which rolls at 17:00 New York), round numbers (distance to the nearest 50-pip-like level), **price patterns on 15-minute and 60-minute bars** (last completed bar's body and wick shares, range vs its recent median, bull / bear engulfing, inside bar, signed run length; fractal swing highs and lows confirmed 3 bars later and the distance to the last of each, the HH/HL structure state; the latest UNFILLED bullish and bearish fair-value gap and its age; an order-block proxy = the last opposite candle before a displacement bar), the macro event calendar (hours to and since CPI, NFP, FOMC, any), the daily macro regime with a one-day lag (VIX rank and change, 10y-2y slope change, dollar index change, VIX9D / VIX, SKEW rank), crypto-only daily series for the crypto class (fear and greed, its 3-day change, stablecoin supply 7-day change, BTC funding), and cross-sectional ranks of the 1-hour and 4-hour return inside the class. 60-minute bars enter only when completed (end time <= decision time). Real ticks are still not usable (they start 2025-10-01).

## 3. Search, test, environment
Same machinery as v5. The entry grid spacing is H bars, the greedy one-position-at-a-time rule is used by the environment, the environment trades on the 15-minute bars (stop checked with the bar high / low of the bid for a long and of the ask for a short, entry at the next bar open, exit at a bar close; the 15-minute bar frame is exact for these rules because every decision sits on a bar close), carry in the environment is zero (swap-free), the flat conservative carry stays in the statistical test.
The agent explores all four classes (idx 6, fx 28, cmd 4, crypto 8), a few thousand rules per generation, and keeps adapting its operator weights from the environment score.

## 4. Calibration (before the real run)
Placebo worlds: every 15-minute bar of a class (all its OHLC, its M1-derived return statistics, its gap) shuffled by one common permutation of the class calendar; spreads, bid-ask offsets, activity counts and the calendar and daily macro series stay with their timestamps (real). The patterns (swings, FVG, order blocks, candles) are recomputed on the shuffled bars, so any 'pattern edge' found there is false by construction. Gate: 0 S1 and 0 S2 in each of 3 never-seen worlds (seeds 31, 32, 33), 20 generations x 2,000 rules, environment scoring on. If it fails, it is reported and the real run does not start.
