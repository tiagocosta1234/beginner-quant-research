# Pre-registration: the frequent-trading trader on real ticks (written 2026-10-13, before any HF modelling ran)

the trader: "we want a frequent trading trader"; on the plan (pull ticks, build a passive-execution test): "yes, pull the ticks and build it".

## Facts that shape the design (stated so nobody reads more into the result than is there)
1. Real MT5 broker ticks exist only from 2025-10-01 (pulled read-only through the MetaTrader5 Python package from the portable tester terminal, whose saved login is the live account <acct>; algorithmic trading disabled, only initialize / terminal_info / account_info / symbols_get / symbol_info / copy_ticks_range / shutdown used; files data\ticks\<SYMBOL>\<YYYY-MM>.parquet: time_msc UTC, bid, ask, flags). The tick era is therefore 2025-10-01..2026-10-13, about 12.5 months. For a high-frequency family that is enough observations (millions of decisions) although it is a single regime of the market.
2. **A correction of what I said earlier**: passive limit orders do NOT earn the spread on an MT5 dealer-model account. A buy limit executes when the ASK falls to the limit price, so after the fill the bid is one spread below; the limit chooses the entry price level, it does not remove the cost. Limit entries are tested as conditional entries (e.g. buy after a small dip) with the exact fill rule below, and adverse selection is whatever the data says.
3. Break-even information coefficients at 1 and 5 minutes on market orders are 0.2-0.6 (class table in the 2026-10-13 discussion; HF_FEASIBILITY), which is why this family also tests limit entries and microstructure signals.

## Family, windows and the new seal
Universe: 12 liquid markets in 4 classes: fx (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF), cmd (XAUUSD), idx (SP, NSDQ, DOW, DAX), crypto (BTCUSD). The other 34 symbols are pulled but not used in the first pass.
Windows inside the tick era: A = 2025-10-01..2026-02-28 (training), B = 2026-03-01..2026-05-31 (early stopping and threshold), VAL = 2026-06-01..2026-08-15 (one look per cell), **HOLD for THIS family = 2026-08-16..2026-10-13, never loaded**. (The 2026 seal of the slow families concerns different strategies and is untouched; this family's seal is its own.) No decision, entry or exit at server hours 23 and 0 (rollover; PREREG_FREEFORM Amendment 5). Macro-event windows are NOT excluded: they are information, and the model sees the minutes to and since each event.

## Decisions, information and targets
A decision every minute at second 0 (UTC), using only ticks up to that instant: mid-price momentum over 1, 5, 15, 60, 300, 900, 3600, 14400 seconds (normalised by the realised tick volatility of the last hour), realised variance over 5 / 60 / 300 s, quote-update counts over 5 / 60 / 300 s relative to their hourly median, spread now (bp), its change over 60 s and its ratio to the hourly median, counts of bid-up, bid-down, ask-up, ask-down moves over 60 s and the signed imbalance, distance to the day's high and low so far, time (hour, minute, weekday), minutes to and since each macro event (CPI, NFP, FOMC, any; capped at 240). About 45 numbers.
Execution (identical in all windows): decision at T; the order reaches the server 300 ms later (an assumed latency; a 1-second sensitivity is reported). Market order: long at the ask / short at the bid of the quote valid at T + 300 ms; exit at the opposite side of the quote valid H minutes after the entry (+300 ms), H in {1, 5, 15, 60}. Limit entry: buy limit at L = bid(T) - d, sell limit at L = ask(T) + d, d in {0, 1 bp}, valid for 60 s after T + 300 ms; a buy fills at the first tick whose ask <= L, filled at L (no price improvement credited), a sell at the first tick whose bid >= L, filled at L; unfilled orders cancel at no cost; after the fill the position exits at market H minutes later. No swap, no commission (the account is swap-free, no commission). 3 execution types x 4 horizons x 4 classes = 48 cells.

## Models and tests (fixed now)
XGBoost per cell with the settings of PREREG_ML.md (squared error, depth 5, eta 0.05, up to 400 trees with early stopping on B, subsample 0.7, column 0.5, min child weight 200, L2 10). Market cells: target = gross mid-to-mid return of the H minutes over its tick volatility (winsorised at +/-5), trade the side of the prediction when |prediction| >= tau. Limit cells: two models (long, short) on the FILLED orders only, target = net return of the filled order; place the order when the predicted net >= tau. tau is the quantile of the prediction on B (50, 70, 80, 90, 95%) that maximises the net mean on B with at least 2,000 trades. Trades are non-overlapping per market (no new order while a position or a pending order of that market exists).
Scores on VAL: net mean bp per trade, number of trades, number of calendar-day clusters, and the gross excess over random timing (same market, side and horizon; cluster-robust daily z, Student t one-sided p). A cell passes (S_HF) only if on VAL: >= 2,000 trades, >= 20 day-clusters, net mean > 0, and p < 0.05 / 48. A pass is then run through the MT5 broker environment (stage E of PREREG_V5_PROP.md, adapted to a trade list) before anything else, with the sealed HOLD of this family looked at once as the clean confirmation.

## Calibration (before the real run)
Three never-seen placebo worlds (seeds 61, 62, 63): the identical pipeline with the labels (all targets of a row) circularly shifted by a random offset (same offset for all markets, at least 3 days) while features stay, so no feature-target relation exists but all cost, spread, activity and fill structure is real. Gate: 0 cells pass in every world; the maximum VAL net mean and excess z of the worlds are reported as the noise ceiling.

## Expectations written now
Net profit per trade must exceed the spread (about 0.5 to 4 bp depending on the class) after 300 ms of latency; microstructure signals in liquid FX and index CFDs are known to be real but small and to live at sub-second horizons on much cheaper execution than ours. The most likely outcome is no passing cell. If a cell passes, its trade frequency (hundreds a day) makes the MT5 broker daily-loss and guard rules, not the edge, the binding constraint.

Calibration gate: PASSED (2026-10-13 08:50). Placebo worlds 61, 62, 63: 0 of 48 cells pass in every world; noise ceiling over the 144 placebo cells: max VAL net +0.41 bp per trade, max VAL excess z 2.43. The real run follows with the same code; the family's HOLD (2026-08-16 onwards) is not loaded.
