# Pre-registration: gold opening-range breakout at the London and New York opens, frequent and 1% risk (family I, two trials) - inspired by the Comet / Perplexity search

Written 2026-10-09 21:48 local (local clock), BEFORE any simulation of these rules. Ledger 60 before; trials 61 (I1, London) and 62 (I2, New York). the trader (2026-10-09): "it has to trade often, risk 1%, and be very profitable. use the browser agent to look for inspiration". Standing constraints: no news / event trading, swap-free, nothing live without the trader's explicit go.

## What the search found (Comet: Perplexity answer, Google, fxabsolute.com research, algotrader.ch)
- The best-documented independent test (fxabsolute.com, 2026-08-29): 20 mechanical XAUUSD strategies currently popular on YouTube (ICT / Smart Money, killzones, M5 scalps), 1.87M M1 candles 2021-01..2026-04, 1% risk, 1.5 ATR stop, 2R target, $0.30 spread + $0.07 commission + $0.05 stop slippage per ounce: 0 of 20 significant after Benjamini-Hochberg, 13 worse than random entries, best -3.8% return vs +142% buy and hold; the correlation between round-trip cost as a share of one risk unit and expectancy is -0.86; M5 scalps start about 16% of R behind. Only the H1 previous-day high / low breakout continuation (+0.007 R) and Power of 3 (+0.003 R) finished positive, and they are not frequent. At a $0.15 spread 3 of 20 are positive; at $0.60 none.
- The same site's DESCRIPTIVE statistic for the gold 30-minute opening range (13:30 UTC, 1,357 days): first-break continuation to +1.0R beyond the box before the opposite side is hit 56.2% of resolved paths (73% at 0.5R, 84% at 0.25R), but 31% of paths never resolve inside 4 hours and costs were not modelled. Taken at face value a 1:1 breakout with a stop at the opposite side of the box has +0.12 R before the unresolved paths and the costs.
- MT5 broker's costs are lower than that study's: median gold spread 0.21 USD (0.5 bp), no commission, and gold's M15 ATR has gone from 2.1 USD (2023) to 11.3 USD (2026), which cuts the cost share of R. That is the one structural reason a frequent gold rule could work in the current regime, and the reason it may stop working if volatility falls.
- Honest reading of the demand: "frequent, 1% risk, super profitable" needs roughly +0.3 R net per trade at 3+ trades a day (about +1% a day) to pass 8% in a month. Nothing in the public evidence reaches that; the strongest documented structure is worth +0.05 to +0.1 R before costs. These two trials test whether the most concrete, daily-frequency idea from the search survives MT5 broker costs; the prior is about 4% to pass the protocol and under 1% to reach the "super profitable" tier.

## Rule (identical for I1 and I2; only the clock differs)
Data: MT5 broker XAUUSD+ M1 bid / ask bars, server clock Europe/Athens converted to real time zones. I1: box = the 30 M1 bars starting at 08:00 Europe/London; I2: box = the 30 bars starting at 09:30 America/New_York. Box high H = max bid high, box low L = min bid low; R = H - L (skip the day if a bar is missing, if R <= 0 or fewer than 28 box bars exist). Window = the 240 minutes after the box ends.
Orders (placed at the box end, one-cancels-other): BUY STOP at H and SELL STOP at L. Long fill when the ask high reaches H: price = max(H, ask open of that bar) + 0.05 USD slippage; short fill when the bid low reaches L: price = min(L, bid open) - 0.05. If both sides trigger inside the same M1 bar the trade is booked as a loss of 1 R plus costs (no award to the preferred side). After a fill the other order is cancelled. Stop = the opposite side of the box (long: L; short: H), filled at the stop level or the bar's open if gapped, 0.05 USD adverse slippage; target = entry + 1.0 R (long) / entry - 1.0 R (short), filled at the target; stop first when a bar touches both; time exit at the window end at the bid (long) / ask (short) open of the next bar. At most one trade per session-day. 1% risk per trade means lot = 1% of equity / (R in USD x 100 oz); results are in R and, for the 1% sizing, in percent of equity.
Statistics: trades, win rate, mean R, profit factor on R, daily t (mean R per trading day / (sd / sqrt(days))), cost share of R (mean (spread + 2 slippage) / R) by year, the equity curve at 1% risk (return, max drawdown), trades per month, and the correlation of the daily P&L with the live pair's guarded daily P&L over 2025-10..2026-09 (clean_boot_lib series MT5 broker x1.2).
Periods: DEV 2022-01-01..2024-12-31, VAL 2025, HOLD-OUT 2026-01-01..2026-09-30 (one look, only if DEV and VAL pass).

## Acceptance (per trial, Bonferroni 62)
1. DEV: >= 400 trades, mean R > +0.05, PF >= 1.10, daily t >= 2.5. 2. VAL 2025: mean R > 0, PF >= 1.05, daily t >= 1.0. 3. HOLD-OUT 2026: mean R > 0. 4. Gate for use: correlation with the pair <= 0.3, clean-simulation uplift in P(pass) with at most 1 point of extra breach, MT5 broker tester validation, AccountGuard-compatible (the 2.0% day stop would stop 2 consecutive losing 1% trades).
"Super profitable" tier (the trader's wording): DEV and VAL mean R >= +0.30 net. If DEV fails the trial is closed, no hold-out.
