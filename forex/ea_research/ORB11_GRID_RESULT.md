## Reading for the trader
The breakout entry is about 0.02-0.04 R better than a random entry with the same stop and target, at every stop and target, and the spread costs more than that. Moving the stop and target redistributes the same small edge between win rate and payoff; it cannot create the missing edge. What made a few cells look good in 2022-25 (US indices at the New York open) is the 2022 trending market already seen this morning, and it is negative in 2026.
