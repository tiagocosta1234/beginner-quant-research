# PREREG_TRAP12: the "failed break at the 12 EMA" setup the trader drew on 22 Oct, as a mechanical rule on MT5 broker prices (written 22 Oct 2026 ~15:5x, local clock; before any result)

Origin: the trader 22 Oct, NY session on OANDA:XAUUSD 1m: he rejected the white 14:30 candle ("that white candle isnt strong, big wick above the close"), drew a short from the 14:32 close with the target on an untouched 1-minute gap (box KSGE3H, `S1_NY_DEMO_22OCT.md`, `manual_22oct_ny_manualbox.py`, `manual_22oct_ny_whybox.py`), called it "literally one of the best 12 ema setups" and then said "now backtest the setup idea". Earlier mechanical forms of his setup all failed on MT5 broker prices (12 EMA family 2,044 trials, MANUALRULE, EMA1000, SWEEP36 0 of 36, DISP24 0 of 24); in those the stop sat about 1 ATR away and MT5 broker's spread cost 0.2-0.3 R a trade. Two things are new here: (1) the context he added (a pullback coil on the EMA, a weak false break through it, a displacement back, his rule that the close cannot sit in an untouched part of a gap) and (2) a target on a whole untouched gap with the stop derived from it, several ATR away, so the spread is a smaller share of the risk. Honest note: the thresholds below are read off ONE example (his 22 Oct box), chosen after seeing it win; no other day was looked at and nothing was run before this file. Tick volume is not part of the rule (the MT5 broker history in `forex/data_mt5` has none).

## Data and periods
MT5 broker M1 bid / ask for all 46 markets (`forex/data_mt5`), DEV 2022-01-01..2024-12-31, VAL 2025-01-01..2025-12-31; nothing after 2025-12-31 is loaded. Server clock = local + 2 h; session opens: London 08:00 London time, NY 09:30 New York time (`ema12_test.open_min`, server minutes, as in DISP24 / SWEEP36).

## The rule (short; the long is the mirror image). Candles are 1-minute MID candles; EMA = EMA 12 of the mid close; ATR(k) = mean high-low range of the 14 candles before k.
Candles t-11 .. d+1 must be consecutive minutes of the same day.
1. **Down-trend:** EMA(t-1) < EMA(t-11).
2. **Pullback coil on the EMA:** each of the 3 candles t-3, t-2, t-1 closes at or below the EMA, within 1.0 ATR(t) of it, with a body of at most 0.5 ATR(t).
3. **False break (trap) candle t:** a white candle (close > open) that opens at or below EMA(t-1), closes above EMA(t), with an upper wick of at least 0.5 x its body (weak close).
4. **Displacement candle d = t+1:** opens at or above EMA(t), closes below EMA(d) and below the trap's low; body >= 1.0 ATR(d); lower (close-side) wick <= 0.3 x body; body > the sum of both wicks.
5. **the trader's close rule (his words: "the close cant be inside an unfilled gap", "even if the gap is mitigated you cant use a close that's still on the rest of the area that hasnt been touched"):** a 1-minute gap is a three-candle FVG (bullish: low(k) > high(k-2); bearish: high(k) < low(k-2)), from the last 240 candles; its untouched remainder = the gap minus the high-low ranges of the candles after its third candle and before the candle being judged. The signal candle's close must not lie in any untouched remainder.
6. **Signal candle s:** s = d if d's close passes rule 5; otherwise s = c = d+1 if c is a continuation candle (high below EMA(c), close below d's close, pullback high(c) - close(d) <= 0.5 x d's body) and c's close passes rule 5; otherwise no setup.
7. **Entry:** market at the open of candle s+1 (sell at the bid, buy at the ask). The entry candle must open inside the window: [session open, session open + W] minutes, W = 15 (the rules card: 08:00:00-08:15:59) or 60. One trade per day and session: the first setup (either side) whose entry falls in the window and which has a target.
8. **Target:** the nearest WHOLE untouched gap in the trade direction (for a short: a bullish gap below, which no candle after its third one has reached, up to and including s), from the last 240 candles, whose near edge is at least 1.0 ATR(s) from the entry. No such gap = no setup (the scan goes on to the next candle).
9. **Exits (D = distance from the entry to the gap's near edge):** "drawn" = stop D, target D (1:1, as his box); "card" = stop 2D, target D (0.5 R, the rules card); "S1" = stop 2D, target 2D (1.0 R, the live plan's bracket). Stop checked first when both are touched in one candle; a short exits at the ask, a long at the bid; open after 240 candles = closed at market.

## Variants: 12 = session (London, NY) x window (15, 60) x exit (drawn, card, S1). Kinds per variant: r = the rule on real bid / ask; i = the inverted side (same distances); c = control: the displacement candle of rule 4 alone (no trend, coil, trap, close rule or continuation), entry after it, same target and exits (what the context adds); z / zc = rule / control at mid prices (no spread, a ceiling).

## Bars (fixed now)
Pooled over the 46 markets per variant, day-clustered t: DEV n >= 300 trades, DEV mean R >= +0.03, DEV t >= 2.64, VAL mean R > 0 and t >= 1.65, every DEV year mean R > 0, best market removed: mean R > 0 and t >= 2.0. XAUUSD alone per variant (his market): DEV n >= 60, mean R >= +0.10, DEV t >= 2.64, VAL mean R > 0. 24 tests in all (12 pooled + 12 gold), one-sided Bonferroni 0.1 / 24 -> t 2.64. A pooled or a gold pass is a candidate; a candidate earns only one look at data this rule has not seen (MT5 broker 2026-01-01..01-31 and 2026-10-01..10-16, with the trader's OK; the sealed 2026-02-01..09-30 belongs to blind batch 2) and then the forward log; nothing goes near the account on a backtest alone, and the S1 live plan is unchanged.

(1) truncation test: signals recomputed with every candle after the entry minute removed must match; (2) hand check: the 22 Oct NY example on his OANDA bars (`MANUAL_22OCT_NY_BARS.csv`, spread 0.55) must give a short with entry at the 14:33 open and the target on the 13:02-13:04 gap's top edge 4062.915; (3) setups per year (XAUUSD and pooled) and the share where s = c; (4) coverage of his April-May 2025 recorded trades (descriptive). If the counts make a variant meaningless (for example fewer than 60 gold trades in DEV for every W = 15 variant), that is reported, not fixed: no threshold changes after this file except a written amendment before any P&L.

## Honest prior
About 85-90% no candidate: every earlier mechanical form failed, the thresholds come from one winning example, and the pattern is rare, so power is low; what could differ is the wider, structure-based stop (smaller spread share) and the gap target.
