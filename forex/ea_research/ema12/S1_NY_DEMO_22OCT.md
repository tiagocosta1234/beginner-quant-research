**Rules, fixed before the window (the same reading as `manual_22oct_rule_setup.py`):**
- Window = time of the MARKET ENTRY, 14:30:00-14:45:59 local (NY cash open 09:30 ET = 14:30 local until the UK clocks go back on 25 Oct); one trade, no re-entry.
- Setup candle = the last closed 1-minute candle: it opens on one side of the EMA 12 and closes on the other, body larger than its bigger wick,
  not small (body >= 1.0 x ATR14, ATR14 = 14-bar mean true range).
- Direction = the side it closed on. Target (card) = the nearest FVG edge or nearby swing high / low at least 1 ATR beyond the entry; card stop = twice that distance.
- **S1:** same stop, take profit at 1.0 R (target distance = stop distance). SL and TP attached at entry, never moved.
- Entry price = the market price at the moment Claude calls it (the paper fill the trader gets is the record if it differs).
- Size for the demo = the MT5 broker sizer's lots for the stop distance (risk budget USD 832 = 0.25 x 3,326), so the R result maps onto the account.
- No setup by 14:45:59 = no trade today.
- The result is read from the OANDA 1m bars on his chart; the MT5 broker gold feed can differ minute by minute, so it is only a side check.
