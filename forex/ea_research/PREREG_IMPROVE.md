the trader: "then we need to get better". Both tests are planning simulations on the installed system (pair x1.5 + ManualDip 500 x0.75, guard 1,500, CPI news flat, pass70 / replay rules, FRESH account, MIX of the MT5 broker 12-month and FT 2022-26 pools); nothing goes live from this round.
## IM1 all small legs together
Legs (each uncorrelated with the pair, daily correlation about 0): RSI(2) US dips, 500 USD day risk, stop 3 sigma, x0.75 (rsi2_leg.py); strategy 3 (overnight-bias ORB) at 0.2 NQ with a 1.67-point spread floor and 2 points of slippage (the real-tick tester put it between 1 and 3); ManualDip at 1,000 instead of 500. Configurations: A installed; B + RSI(2); C + strategy 3; D + both; E + both + ManualDip x2. Statistic: P(pass) within 22 / 44 / 60 business days and the breach by 60. Accept a configuration as an improvement if its P(pass) within 44 business days is at least 3 points above A with the breach by 60 days not more than 1 point higher.
## IM2 performance throttle (size by the system's own recent P&L)
Reference signal: the system's own x1.5 daily P&L over the previous 40 business days (strictly before). Rule T1: size x1.5 when that sum is > 0, x1.0 otherwise; rule T2: x2.0 when > 0, x1.0 otherwise (the day's V, B, Hi taken from the series of the chosen multiplier, as RS1). Empirical replay from every start day (fresh account), pass and breach within 22 / 44 business days against the unthrottled system. Accept as an improvement if pass within 44 bd is higher in BOTH pools and the breach is not higher in either.
Caveats fixed in advance: in-sample pools; the 12-month pool has almost no OFF days (2), so IM2 rests on the FT pool; no hold-out exists for these planning tests.
