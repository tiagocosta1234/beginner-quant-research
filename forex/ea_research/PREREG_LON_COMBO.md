# PREREG_LON_COMBO - MT5 broker evaluation cycles of the 7 kept London per-symbol rules, alone and in all 127 combinations (written 2026-10-15 local clock 21:35, before running)
the trader: "check the 2026 evaluation cycle stats for each separate symbol, then do every combination possible of the symbols and test them together".
Rules: the 7 LON_PERSYM rules kept because positive in 2025 and 2026 (DAX, DOW, EURUSD, GBPCAD, NIKKEI, USDJPY, XAUUSD; each with its own trigger / filter / exit). Daily R per symbol from ORB11_ENTRY (MT5 broker bid/ask, 2022-26) and from HistData 2020-21 (today's MT5 broker London spread; no DOW; DAX only to 2020-06-12).
MT5 broker cycle (best3m_cycle.cycle): 100,000 start, target 108,000 counted, floor 92,000, 2,500 daily line from the day-start balance, +2,000 a day counted; daily line checked OPTIMISTIC (day net) and PESSIMISTIC (sum of the day's losing trades). Risk per trade X = 250, 500, 1,000 USD, the same on every symbol.
Starts: every business day; reported: pass and breach within 30 calendar days (starts at least 30 days before the data end) for starts in 2026 (Jan..Aug), 2022-25 and 2020-21.
BIAS WARNING written before running: the 7 symbols were kept BECAUSE their rules were positive in 2026 (and 2025), so 2026 cycles are in-sample by construction and the best of 127 combinations on 2026 is a further selection. Only the 2020-21 cycles (never used to choose anything) and, weaker, 2022-25 (used to choose the rule) say anything about the future. No acceptance bar; a combination is called promising only if it also passes more than it breaches in 2020-21.
