# Breakout knowledge (MT5 broker, 2022-2026) - what one research day established (2026-10-15, updated 14:25 local clock)
Written for the trader after the breakout research day (`BREAKOUT_DAY_2026-10-15.md`, rounds 1-32 + the morning's TRB / ORB rounds). Every number is net of MT5 broker's real bid/ask spread, causal (no look-ahead; truncation tests passed), in R = the distance from entry to stop. "DEV / VAL / 2026" = 2022-24 / 2025 / 2026-01..10-02. About 200 pre-registered cells today (95 of them in the hour-of-day map). On MT5 broker data 0 passed; ONE lead, the USDJPY London-open breakout, then passed a pre-registered out-of-time check on 2020-21 data (section 2). That, and WHY everything else fails, is the headline.

## 1. The facts
1. **A breakout carries a little real information, and it is smaller than the spread.** Over ~54,000 session breakouts on 46 markets, a breakout entry beats a random entry at the same moment by 0.03-0.05 R a trade (it reaches +1R first 43% of the time vs 40%), while one spread costs 0.04-0.22 R. Net: -0.03 to -0.08 R a trade on M1 bars, about 0.03 R worse on MT5 broker real ticks (all 46 markets); the information over random entries survives on ticks (+0.09 R in Oct 2025 - Oct 2026). (BRK-EXITS, BRK-ANATOMY, TICK_GAP)
2. **94% of breakouts trade back inside the box within an hour** (89% within 15 minutes). Stops inside the box get hit by noise; retest entries pick the weak breakouts (the strong ones never come back); failed-breakout reversals are no better than random. (BRK-ANATOMY, BRK-RETEST)
3. **Breakouts are slow:** the best price of the day comes a median 4 hours after the fill. Holding to the session close is the least bad exit; fixed 1-2R targets, breakeven moves and trailing stops all cut the few big winners. **Adding lots to a winner (pyramiding at +1R / +2R) makes it worse** (-0.02..-0.11 R): moves that reach +1R come back to the raised stop more often than they run. (BRK-EXITS, BRK-PYRAMID)
4. **The edge was a 2022 thing.** Breakout information (breakout minus random) by year: +0.097 R (2022), +0.005, +0.012, -0.018, +0.027 (2026); indices +0.149 (2022) -> -0.045 (2026). On three indices never used today (FTSE, CAC, Russell) the same decay. The 2022 high-volatility bear market trended intraday; since then it does not. (BRK-ANATOMY, INDEX-OOS)
5. **"Every year has its own best breakout" is exactly what luck produces.** The hindsight-best of 2,808 variants per market earns +0.16..+0.25 R a trade every year, a different one each year. 2,808 variants of FIXED quality produce the same (+0.23..+0.28) from noise alone, in 5 of 5 years; even 2,808 equally bad variants crown a "+0.14..+0.23 best" by luck. Chasing the recent winner (1, 3, 6 or 12 months) loses 0.05-0.27 R a trade in every year. Adapting is the right instinct only when there is something real to adapt to; here the yearly winners are noise. (TRB-BEST, TRB-ADAPT, TRB-LUCK, chart `D:\finance\scratch\charts\TRB_LUCK.png`)
6. **Costs decide more than patterns.** Results rise with the box width measured in spreads: boxes under 10 spreads lose 0.15-0.23 R, boxes over 40 spreads earn +0.08 R on MT5 broker in every year 2022-26 but only break even on other brokers' instruments: a necessary condition, not an edge. FX opening boxes shrank from ~28 spreads (2022) to ~14 (2024-26). (BRK-COST, COST-OOS)
7. **No hour of the day works.** 60-minute box breakouts at every hour (95 hour x class cells): negative in 87 of 95 cells in 2022-24; least bad at the big session opens (liquidity), nowhere positive after costs. (BRK-HOURS, chart `BRK_HOURS.png`)
8. **The famous patterns and published effects do not survive MT5 broker costs 2022-26:** bull / bear flags (worse than random: on M5 an impulse plus a tight pause is usually the END of the move), TTM squeeze (= random), round-number crossings (Osler 2003: = any level = random), intraday Donchian / turtle, Larry Williams' volatility breakout, the 5-minute ORB (Zarattini), Crabel inside day, cash gaps, intraday momentum from the first to the last half hour (Gao et al. 2018: gross about zero on MT5 broker indices). (BRK-FLAG, BRK-SQUEEZE, BRK-ROUND, BRK-DONCH, BRK-CLASSICS, BRK-IMOM)
9. **Machine learning finds the losers, not winners.** A walk-forward model with everything known at the entry ranks 986,000 breakouts consistently out of sample (lowest decile -0.33 R, highest about 0), mostly by box width in spreads; its best decile only breaks even. (BRK-META, BRK-ML2)
10. **Filters that sound right do not help:** volume surge at the breakout, busy opening box, volatility regime, NR4/NR7, inside days, trend direction outside indices, relative strength, crowd days, early movers, ML meta-labels (lift to ~0), weekly / monthly boxes, news vs quiet days, weekdays, turn of the month, after options expiry (worse), market-wide climate, relative-spread breakouts, multi-day holds, fresh 20-day breakouts (exhaustion), buffers beyond the level, levels tested several times, a confirming partner market, a lagging partner market (rounds 3-8, 12, 15-20, 31-32)
11. **The first seconds say nothing.** On 11,684 breakouts with MT5 broker real ticks the move, tick activity, spread and order flow in the first 30 seconds do not separate real from false breakouts; the fastest and busiest are among the worst. (BRK-TICKS)
12. **When the first breakout fails, the day chops.** Stop-and-reverse loses 0.20-0.24 R a trade; yesterday's breakout result says nothing about today's. (BRK-SAR)
13. **The card's gold London leg did not "stop working" in 2026:** its yearly means (-0.11..+0.08 R) are all within one standard error of luck (~0.09 R); 2024 looked exactly like 2026; the opposite trade loses every year too. There was no stable edge to lose. (GOLD_ORB_2026_DIAG)

## 3. Practical rules for anyone trading breakouts by hand on MT5 broker
1. Never trade a box narrower than ~25 spreads (check the spread before placing the orders).
2. Put the stop at the other side of the box, not inside it, and expect price to come back to the level.
3. Do not take a fixed 1R target; hold to the session close or use a wide trail. Do not add lots to a winner.
4. Expect a win rate of about 30-40% and losing streaks of 10-12 (March 2026 on Nasdaq: 12 in a row).
5. Size so that a 12-loss streak fits inside the MT5 broker floor buffer.
6. Treat any breakout that "worked great last year" as last year's luck until it has worked again on new data.
7. Be flat before CPI and NFP (13:30 London) and FOMC (19:00 London): MT5 broker widens spreads 10-15x and in more than half of these releases the price jumps past a card-sized stop within 10 seconds (gold: $15 median, $63 on 4 Sep 2026); on real ticks, London-session breakouts held through CPI / NFP lost 0.38 R on those days vs 0.17 R when closed at 13:25 (`NEWS_GAPS_RESULT.md`, `TICK_GAP_RESULT.md`).

## 6. What would be needed to do better
Information the chart does not contain (positioning, order flow, news content) or a cheaper cost structure (raw-spread account with commission where boxes are wide).
