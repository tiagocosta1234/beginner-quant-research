# EURUSD chart-reading practice log (2026-10-14, the trader: "get familiar with analyzing EURUSD with technical analysis, train for a while")
Method: blind cases from MT5 broker EURUSD M1 bid/ask (2022-03..2025-12, 10:00 server cut, 2026 untouched): daily (70 bars) + H1 (120 bars) candles, EMA20/50, prior-day high/low, no dates. Calls (side, stop, target; horizon 24 H1 bars) are written to `calls_N.json` BEFORE `score.py` is run. Stop counts first if both touched in a bar; real bid/ask fills. Files in `eurusd_practice/`.
- Round 1 (10 cases, free discretionary reading, stops 10-30 pips): 8 trades, 3 winners, net -0.43R. Lesson: median H1 range is ~18 pips and the 10:00 bar is the London open; stops of 14-20 pips were hit in the first bar in 4 of 8 trades.
- Round 2 (12 cases, rules fixed first: stop >= 2xATR(H1) min 20 pips, target 1.5x, trade only if daily EMA20/EMA50 order and price vs EMA20 agree, else flat): 5 trades, 2 winners, net 0.00R; 7 flats (price was on the wrong side of the daily EMA20; in 5 of the 7 EURUSD rose, a 12-case observation, untested).
- Total 13 trades: -0.43R. That is too small a sample to say anything about skill (a coin flip with 1.5R winners has sd about 1.4R over 13 trades); the practice teaches process (stop size vs noise, hour of day, trend filter) and does not show an edge, consistent with the quantitative tests (FX3_MAP, FX3_OPEN, FX3_FIT2026).
- Round 3 (20 cases, same fixed rules as round 2): case 3.2 turned out to be the same chart as 1.2 (the random draw repeated 2025-06-04; excluded from the totals). 13 unique trades, 3 winners, net -5.5R; 6 flats. Trend-filtered trades hit the 20-36 pip stop before the 1.5R target in 10 of 13 cases.
- Rule-based rounds 2+3 together: 18 trades, 6 winners (33%), net -5.5R (-0.31R per trade); break-even for a 1.5R target is a 40% hit rate. Nothing suggests the daily-EMA trend filter adds an edge at a 24-hour horizon on EURUSD; this matches the quantitative tests. 18 trades is still a small sample (sd of the mean about 0.5R); the exact question is cheap to settle on all ~1,000 days of 2022-25 (next step: pre-registered bracket test of the same rules, 2026 untouched).
