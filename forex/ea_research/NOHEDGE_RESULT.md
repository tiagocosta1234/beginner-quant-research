# No-hedge rule (2026-10-10 01:34 local; the trader: "(original wording omitted)" / "(original wording omitted)")

Rule (AccountGuard.EnforceNoHedge, ManualSDB input InpGuardNoHedge): per symbol, keep the side whose oldest position was opened first and close every position of the other side within about a second (cost: the spread).
