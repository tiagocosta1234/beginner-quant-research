## Reading
- **The only thing in the sample that makes money is drift, not confluence.** A plain long-only book on the 14 non-FX markets earned a net Sharpe of +1.56 (t +2.74) over 2023-25 (metals +1.78, indices +1.23, crypto +1.07; oil -0.25). Every long-biased result above (C1: 64% of its positions are long) is that drift with extra turnover. A system tested only inside a bull market of gold, indices and bitcoin will look good for the wrong reason; the circular-shift permutation test is the check that catches this, and it caught it (C1 p 0.76 / 0.996).
- **No interaction was found.** The boosting model (depth 3, allowed to build any three-way confluence of 41 features) has a cross-sectional IC of +0.007 (t +0.7) and a time-series IC of +0.024 (t +1.6) on years it had never seen; the shuffled-label control gives IC -0.003, so the pipeline is not leaking. Its best year (2025, gross Sharpe +1.5) is also the year a long-only book earned the most. Its top features by permutation importance are market-wide context (USD proxy 5-day, US500 volatility percentile, weekday, US500 20-day), i.e. it predicts the common market move on a day, not a pattern in one market. With the static per-market bias removed C3a is +0.53 (t +0.94), market-neutral +0.08 (t +0.14): a hint at best, far from established.
- **Trend components were wrong-signed in 2023-25.** Exploratory, market-demeaned, date-clustered: the 120-day trend sign (A) and the 50/200 cross (B) have slopes of -0.034 / -0.031 on the 1-day label (t -2.8 / -2.7) and -0.069 / -0.071 on the 5-day label (t -5.9 / -6.3). That is the same message as `DAILY_TREND_BASKET_RESULT.md` (gross Sharpe -0.5 in 2022-24): no stable trend premium in this window, and "buy with the trend" is the part of a confluence that hurts.
- **Short-term reversal is the only component with the same sign in both windows, and it is not tradable as a portfolio.** RSI(2) < 10 / > 90 (D) has slope +0.07 on the 5-day label (t +2.4 in 2021-22, +2.9 in 2023-25); the 5-day-return component (C) +0.04 / +0.05 (t +1.3 / +1.9). Per signal that is +13 to +23 bp over 5 days before an average 4.7 bp round-trip cost (the effect is largest in oil and crypto, +43 to +167 bp, smallest in FX, +6 to +7 bp). But as a portfolio (equal-risk, costed) the RSI(2) reversal returns net Sharpe +0.49 (t +0.9) over 2023-25 and about 0 in 2021-22 (-0.18 to +0.17 for holds of 1-5 days); the pairwise t of 2-3 comes from pooling many signals that fire on the same market-wide sell-off days, which carry far less independent information than the row count suggests. This is the "buy the dip" lead of the Broker campaign again (real-looking, too small and too clustered to matter).
- **Confluence buys confidence, not size.** Requiring three reversal conditions to agree (C+D+E = 3) gives a POOL net Sharpe of +0.10 against +0.49 for RSI(2) alone: stacking removed trades without adding edge, as in round 174.
- Costs are not the reason: the gross Sharpe of the best trial is only +0.94, mostly drift, and the cost assumptions were generous round numbers (1.2-8 bp one way). Changing them would not move any trial across a t of 2.4.

## What this means for the account
Nothing here changes the live plan; it adds to the case that the gold pair (SDB + XAU5) remains the only confirmed edge, and that the yardstick "a long-only gold-and-silver book earned a net Sharpe near 1.8 over 2023-25" must be kept in mind when the pair's 2025-26 record is read (its edge was measured while gold rallied; the clean-simulation regime mix already allows for a flat gold). The daily panel cannot speed up the MT5 broker test anyway (a daily Sharpe near 1.4 would be the best possible outcome and the pass-speed gate needs about 5).
