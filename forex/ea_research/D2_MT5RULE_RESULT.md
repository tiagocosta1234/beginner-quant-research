## The rule that changes the answer
MT5 broker (Daily Drawdown article 11769446, read today): loss level = day-start BALANCE - 2,500; breach if equity or balance touches it. For a book that holds positions for weeks, floating losses carried from earlier days count against today's line, floating profit gives no room, and profit counts only when realised, at most +2,000 a day (MT5 broker Pro removes the rest at rollover).

## Verdict
- **HOLD with a profit skim** (close the book when its floating profit is >= 1,800 at 22:30 server, re-enter at the next business day 18:00; guard at day-start balance - 1,500; pass lock): on 181 rolling historical starts (2022-01 .. 2025-06, every 5th business day, overlapping) at 100k gross: pass / breach by 22 Dec 0.0 / 1.1%, 31 Mar 27 29.8 / 3.3%, 30 Jun 27 59.7 / 4.4%. At 150k: 2.2 / 12.2, 27.6 / 25.4, 49.7 / 35.4. At 75k: 0 / 0, 11.0 / 0, 33.7 / 0.
- **Strongly regime dependent:** 2022 starts (bear market): 3 pass, 4 breach, 45 still running after 185 business days (the 200-day filter keeps the book mostly flat); 2023: 29 / 3 / 20; 2024: 50 / 1 / 0; 2025: 26 / 0 / 0. The rolling sample is weighted to the 2023-25 bull markets: read 60% as the good-regime case.
- **Without the skim it never passes** (0.0% at 75k / 100k, 1.7% at 150k by 30 Jun 27, with 10-47% breach): unrealised profit does not count, and realising it in one day counts only 2,000. **So D2 on MT5 broker Pro works only if MT5 broker accepts closing the whole book at about +1,800 and re-opening it the next day.** MT5 broker's rules forbid splitting or re-opening trades to get around the 2% daily cap; whether a next-day re-entry of the same weekly book counts as that is a question for MT5 broker support (the trader's step), to be answered in writing before any use.
- **DAILY mode (open 01:05, close 22:45, flat at every rollover) is not usable:** at 100k gross +14 USD a day (vs about +45 held: most of the drift is earned overnight and over weekends), 2022 -11.8k, 2026 +0.7k; bootstrap 30 Jun 27 24 / 32-39%, rolling starts 25 / 14%; breach >= pass at every size.
- Over 2022-01..2026-10 without stopping (100k, HOLD with skim): counted balance change by year -11.2k, +9.5k, +19.2k, +23.3k, -0.1k (2026); 59 guard closes and 70 skims; worst counted day -1,753; no day at the -2,500 line.
- Skim threshold (descriptive, 100k, rolling starts, 30 Jun 27): 1,200 -> 59.1 / 2.2, 1,500 -> 50.3 / 4.4, 1,800 -> 59.7 / 4.4, 1,950 -> 55.8 / 4.4: no sharp optimum; what matters is that profit is realised in pieces below 2,000.
- The gold EA pair's simulator (clean_guard_sim) already marks each trade from its entry price and counts only closed P&L, so the pair's numbers respect the balance-based line (still in-sample).
- Engine check: with every rule switched off the HOLD engine reproduces the minute engine (2022-24 at 100k: +23,218 vs +23,697).
