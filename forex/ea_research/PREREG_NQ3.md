# Pre-registration: the three Nasdaq strategies of the "Matio" video, causal test on MT5 broker data (family NQ3) (written 2026-10-12, before any MT5 broker trade of them was computed)

Source: `youtube_ideas/transcrito.txt` (video "I Asked a Hedge Fund Manager His TOP 3 Strategies", transcript pasted by the trader). Claims made in the video: each strategy alone has about 40% pass probability, the three together pass in about 5 trading days at 5 micro contracts each, correlation between them < 0.25; "2018 .. 2022 in-sample, 2023 onwards out-of-sample", parameters (30% of ATR, 800 / 1,500 USD, 3 trades a day) optimised in sample. Per-trade claims: S1 win about 67% (RR 0.53), average trade +112 USD on 1 NQ.
the trader's scope rule: data from 2020 onward. The authors' own out-of-sample start is 2023, so 2022 is reported but does NOT decide.

## Instrument and data
MT5 broker NSDQ (Nasdaq 100 index CFD, stands in for NQ futures; 1 NQ = 20 USD per point, so USD figures are "per 1 NQ"), MT5 broker M1 bid/ask 2021-12 .. 2026-10-02, swap-free. All clocks are America/Chicago (CT); "midnight" is 00:00 CT. Entries at the ask (long) / bid (short), exits at the bid (long) / ask (short); ask = bid + the spread of the bar's open minute; TP/SL are checked minute by minute on M1 (bid high = max(bid-open, bid-close) of the minute, a small underestimate; bid low = bl), stop checked before target in the same minute, a gap through the stop exits at the open.
Declared deviations (no volume in the MT5 broker M1 files; ambiguities in the transcript, each resolved the simple way and fixed here):
1. VWAP is replaced by a TWAP: cumulative mean of the minute typical price (bid-high + bid-low + bid-close) / 3 since 00:00 CT (equal volume per minute).
2. A15 = mean of the true ranges of the 15 sessions BEFORE today (session = 00:00-16:00 CT; TR with the previous session close), known at midnight. The video says "moving average of daily ATR over 15"; Wilder ATR(14) smoothing is not applied.
3. ADX is Wilder ADX(14) on the strategy's own bar size (1 min for S2, 15 min for S3); bar highs are the bid-high proxy.
4. S2 may trade more than once a day (one position at a time, re-armed by a new close above the opening-range high after each exit), entries from 9:00 to 15:50 CT; its TP is the highest bid-high of the 5 bars up to the signal bar, SL the lowest bid-low of the 20, both fixed at entry.
5. S3 overnight range = 23:00 CT (previous calendar day) .. 08:30 CT; entries until 14:15 CT.

## The three rules
S1 VAULT BREAK (30 min, long only): level N = open(00:00 CT bid-open) + 0.30 x A15. A 30-minute bar (CT clock) that closes above N AND above the TWAP, with the NEXT bar's open between 10:00 and 14:30 CT: buy at that open (ask). At most 3 entries per session, one position at a time. TP +40 points (800 USD), SL -75 points (1,500 USD), forced exit at 14:30 CT.
S2 VWAP PULLBACK + ADX (1 min, long only): opening range = 08:30-09:00 CT high. After 09:00: armed when a 1-min bar closes above the range high; then a bar whose low touches the TWAP; then a bar that closes above the TWAP with ADX > 20 and ADX not greater than the previous bar's: buy at the next bar's open. TP = highest high of the last 5 bars, SL = lowest low of the last 20; forced exit at 15:55 CT.
S3 OVERNIGHT BIAS ORB (15 min, long and short, one trade a day): bias from the 08:30 open inside the overnight range (>= 2/3 long, <= 1/3 short, else no trade); opening range = the 08:30-08:45 bar; the first 15-minute bar from 08:45 that closes beyond the range in the bias direction with ADX > 20: enter at the next open. SL = 0.30 x A15 points, TP = 3 x SL, forced exit at 14:30 CT.

## Windows and acceptance (by entry date, per strategy)
OUT-OF-SAMPLE (the authors' own) DEV = 2023-01-01 .. 2024-12-31, VAL = 2025, HOLD-OUT = 2026-01-01 .. 2026-09-30 (one look, accepted strategies only); 2022 reported, not used.
Accept: DEV >= 40 trades, mean >= +30 USD per trade (1 NQ) after the spread (a quarter of the video's S1 claim), PF >= 1.2, one-sided t >= 1.65 (day-averaged); VAL mean > 0; HOLD mean > 0.
Portfolio (informative, not a trial): the three at 0.5 NQ each (5 micro, 10 USD per point), daily series through the same pass70 simulator, guard 1,500, fresh account, MIX of the MT5 broker 12-month and FT 2022-26 day sets: P(pass) within 22 / 44 business days against the installed system (17.4% / 47.5%), and their daily correlations.
Three trials (S1, S2, S3). Nothing goes live from this round; a pass is a candidate for an EA build, a real-tick MT5 broker tester run and the guard rules.

## Honest prior
Low. The video's numbers come from a futures backtest of the author on a 2018-2026 NQ series with parameters tuned in sample; S1 pays 800 against a 1,500 stop (needs a win rate above 65% to break even), S2 and S3 are variants of opening-range / VWAP ideas that failed or were flat in our noise-area and gap tests. The "5 days to pass at 5 micro" claim cannot be right on arithmetic alone: 5 micro on NQ is 10 USD per point, an 8,000 USD target needs 800 points of net gain in a week. Expected: small positive or negative per-trade means that the spread eats.
