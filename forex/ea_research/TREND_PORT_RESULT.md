## Verdict: no claim and no suggestive cell; trend adds nothing over buy-and-hold in 2021-25
- 15 cells (5 signals x ALL / FX / NONFX), weekly rebalance, vol-parity sizing, MT5 broker half-spreads (FX 0.31 bp, non-FX 4.65 bp), swap-free, net of costs. Sharpe 2021-25: FX trend -0.10..-0.01 (zero); ALL trend +0.08..+0.42; NONFX trend +0.22..+0.63 (best NONFX 20-day, Sharpe 0.63, DEV 0.56 / VAL 0.73, bootstrap p 0.05). At 2x costs the same cells lose 0.0-0.1 Sharpe.
- The yardstick wins: a long-only vol-parity portfolio of the same markets has Sharpe 0.94 (ALL) and 1.03 (NONFX) over 2021-25 (DEV 0.71 / 0.79, VAL 1.27 / 1.37), against 0.4-0.6 for the trend cells; in FX the long-only vol-parity yardstick is 0.51. In this sample the return is market drift (indices, gold, crypto bull markets), not trend timing, the same finding as the daily confluence panel. Trend filters made the portfolio worse than not timing at all.
- Power: five years cannot confirm a Sharpe below about 1.2; the only way to test whether trend works over more regimes is the pre-2020 history, which the trader declined.
