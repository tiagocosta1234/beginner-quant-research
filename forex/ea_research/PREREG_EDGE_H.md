# Pre-registration: the search for a SECOND edge "far greater than the current one" (the trader 2026-10-09 evening: "we need another edge, far greater than our current one") - three structurally different trials (family H)

Written 2026-10-09 21:42 local (local clock), BEFORE any simulation of these rules. Ledger 57 before; trials 58 (H1), 59 (H2), 60 (H3). Budget over the original protocol again; the trader's request is the amendment. Standing constraints: NO news / event trading; swap-free account; crypto stays research-only; nothing goes live without the trader's explicit go.
Why these three (what is different from the 57 earlier trials): they are NOT entry-timing rules on one chart. H1 and H2 are RELATIVE-VALUE trades between two instruments linked by an economic anchor (the gold-silver and Brent-WTI ratios), so they do not need a direction in the market and are largely uncorrelated with the gold pair's long gold beta. H3 is a documented, slow, cross-sectional calendar effect (equity indices earn most of their return between the cash close and the next cash open) tested on six index CFDs, three of which (DAX, NIKKEI, ASX) were exported only today. Data: MT5 broker M1 bid / ask bars (forex/data_mt5), server clock = Europe/Athens.
Honest prior: about 3% each to pass the protocol; "far greater" than the current edge is what the second tier below asks, and the prior for that is about 0.5% each. Reason: the 57 earlier trials, the gold pair is itself in-sample and regime dependent, and any real edge in index overnight drift or in metal / oil ratios is a small number of basis points a trade that spreads, swap-free rollover widening and slippage can eat.

## H1 - gold / silver ratio mean reversion (daily)
Daily bars per server day from the M1 bid / ask file: open = first bar of the day (bid open, ask open), close = last bar's bid close; mid return uses the bid series. Ratio R_d = XAUUSD close / XAGUSD close. z_d = (ln R_d - mean of ln R over the previous 60 days) / sd of the same 60 days, using days up to and including d. Signal at the close of day d: z_d >= +2.0 -> SHORT the ratio (short gold, long silver, equal USD notional at entry); z_d <= -2.0 -> LONG the ratio. Entry at the open of day d+1 (long leg at the ask, short leg at the bid) plus slippage 0.25 USD gold / 0.02 USD silver per leg per side; exit at the open of the first later day with |z| <= 0.5, or after 30 days, or at a stop when |z| >= 4.0 in the entry direction (all evaluated on closes, exit at the next open). One position at a time. Daily P&L of the position = side x (leg-1 return - leg-2 return) on equal notional (open-to-close on the entry day, close-to-close afterwards, close-to-open on the exit day), minus the entry and exit spreads and slippage; no swap (swap-free account).

## H2 - Brent / WTI ratio mean reversion (daily)
Identical to H1 with R_d = BRENT close / WTI close, slippage 0.04 USD per leg per side.

## H3 - overnight index drift (close to open), six indices
For each index and each trading day: LONG from the cash close to the next cash open, i.e. buy at the ask of the M1 bar at the exchange's cash close time and sell at the bid of the M1 bar at the next cash open time (exchange local clock converted with real time zones from the Athens server clock): SP, NSDQ, DOW 16:00 -> 09:30 America/New_York; DAX 17:30 -> 09:00 Europe/Berlin; NIKKEI 15:00 -> 09:00 Asia/Tokyo; ASX 16:00 -> 10:00 Australia/Sydney. Slippage 0.12 bp per side. The Friday close to Monday open counts as one trade (it carries three nights; reported separately as a split). Days without both bars are skipped. Daily series = the equal-weight mean of the net returns of the indices that traded that day; per-index means reported. Control (not a trial): the same indices OPEN -> CLOSE (the daytime complement), long, same costs, to show where the return is earned.

## Statistics and periods
Per trial: trades, mean net bp per trade, win rate, profit factor, daily t (mean of the daily P&L series over ALL days of the period / (sd / sqrt(days)), flat days count as zero for H1 and H2), annualised Sharpe of the daily series, positive share by sub-instrument for H3, correlation of the daily P&L with the live pair's guarded daily P&L over 2025-10..2026-09 (clean_boot_lib series MT5 broker x1.2). Periods: DEV 2022-01-01..2024-12-31, VAL 2025, POOL 2022-2025, HOLD-OUT 2026-01-01..2026-09-30 (one look, only if DEV and VAL pass).

## Acceptance
Tier 1 (the usual protocol, Bonferroni 60): DEV daily t >= 2.5 with mean net P&L > 0 and (H1, H2) at least 25 trades / (H3) at least 1,500 trades; VAL daily t >= 1.0 and mean > 0; HOLD-OUT mean > 0; gate for use: daily correlation with the pair <= 0.3, executable as an EA on MT5 broker (daily or overnight holds allowed, fixed risk, hard stop, AccountGuard-compatible), clean-simulation uplift in P(pass).
Tier 2 ("far greater than the current edge", the trader's wording): DEV daily t >= 4.0 and VAL daily t >= 2.0 and annualised Sharpe of the daily series >= 2.0 net. The current pair's daily t over the last 12 months is the yardstick printed with the results.
If Tier 1 fails in DEV the trial is closed, no hold-out.
