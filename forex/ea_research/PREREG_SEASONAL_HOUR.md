# Pre-registration: sparse hour-of-day x market seasonality with execution at minute 5 (family K3, one rule)

Written 2026-10-10 PC-clock time stamped by the commit that adds this file, BEFORE any run of this rule. Follows K2b (`CONFLUENCE_H1B_OUTPUT.txt`): the four registered hourly models all closed, but a diagnostic on the seasonality model S1b showed (i) a real hour x market structure (circular-shift permutation p 0.001, cell P&L correlated 0.36 between 2024 and 2025) and (ii) that about two thirds of its FX gross P&L came from marking prices at the FIRST minute of the hour (re-marking the same positions at minute 5 cut the gross Sharpe from 3.2 / 4.4 to 1.3 / 2.0 and FX gross from 9,016 to 3,128 bp), while index and crypto cells survived (625 to 664 bp, 1,891 to 1,204 bp). The model also churned (8.5 position changes per day per market), so costs (66 bp/day) swamped the gross (22 bp/day). K3 asks the one clean question left: does a SPARSE set of strongly significant cells, executed at minute 5 and netted across consecutive hours, make money after real quote costs on data nobody has looked at?

## Honesty about the data
The 2024 and 2025 results of K3 are NOT independent evidence: I looked at 2024-25 cell P&L in the K2b diagnostic before writing this rule (the rule, the threshold and the minute-5 execution were chosen after that look). They are reported as "development". The only untouched data is the 2026 hold-out (2026-01-01..2026-09-30, 195 server dates), opened ONCE, only after the development numbers are recorded; it is the decision.

## Data and bars
MT5 broker M1 bid/ask (`forex/data_mt5`), the same 42 markets, 2021-12 onward, same integrity filters and bar rules as K2 (>= 20 minutes in the bar, Monday-Friday server dates, quoted relative spread < 5%). For each hourly bar j: m5_j = mid at the first M1 minute of the bar with minute-of-hour >= 5 (execution and marking price), sp5_j = its relative spread, sigma_j = EWMA(alpha 0.02, min 100 bars) of squared bar-to-bar log returns of the mid close (known at the end of bar j-1 for use in bar j). The bar return of a position held through bar j is r_j = m5_(j+1) / m5_j - 1 and is only defined (the bar is tradable) if the next existing bar starts exactly one hour later (no position is ever held through a closure or a weekend) and the server hour of bar j is in 1..22 (no execution or marking at the 00:05 / 01:05 rollover window of hours 0 and 23).

## Rule (fixed now)
Cell = (market, server hour of the held bar j). Walk-forward by test year Y in {2024, 2025} (development) and 2026 (hold-out): the training window is every tradable bar from 2022-01-01 whose return ends before Jan 1 of Y. For each cell: x = r_j / sigma_j per day (one observation per server date), n = number of days, t = mean(x) / (sd(x) / sqrt(n)), mean_bp = mean of raw r_j in bp, cost_bp = 2 x (0.5 x median sp5 in the cell + 0.1 bp). A cell is SELECTED if n >= 300, |t| >= 4.0 and |mean_bp| >= 1.5 x cost_bp; its position is sign(mean) in every test-year bar of that cell. Weight w_j = pos x 0.0005 / sigma_j (5 bp of per-bar volatility at |pos| = 1, as K2); P&L of bar j = w_j x r_j - |w_j - w_(j-1)| x (0.5 x sp5_j + 0.1 bp), with w_(j-1) the previous bar's weight of the same market (zero if it is not adjacent), so consecutive selected hours with the same sign are netted.
Placebo (sanity, not a trial): the same selection run on training data in which the hour labels are shuffled within each (market, date); the count of selected cells there is the false-positive yardstick.
Statistics: daily sums by server date, net and gross annualised Sharpe (x sqrt 252), daily t, mean bp/day, profit factor, maximum drawdown, selected cells per year and their class mix, trades per day, share of selected cells with positive out-of-sample gross, and the circular-shift permutation (500 random shifts of each market's position series by 24 or more bars inside the test window).

## Acceptance
1. Placebo selects fewer than 10% of the number of cells the real data selects (otherwise the threshold is too lax and the whole rule is closed).
2. Development: net Sharpe > 0 in both 2024 and 2025 AND pooled 2024-25 daily t >= 2.0 AND permutation p <= 0.01 (necessary, not sufficient: contaminated).
3. HOLD-OUT 2026 (one look): net Sharpe >= 0.5 AND daily t >= 1.65 (one-sided 5%) AND at least 60% of the selected cells have positive out-of-sample gross.
4. Gate for any use: re-run on MT5 broker ticks where available (since 2025-09-25) and on a second execution minute (minute 10) with the same cells (the profit must not live on one minute); correlation with the gold pair <= 0.3; uplift in the clean simulation with <= 1 point of extra breach; fixed risk, hard stops, AccountGuard-compatible, small lots (MT5 broker guideline 1% per idea) and no prohibited behaviour (no latency / arbitrage style; a one-hour hold at a fixed clock time is ordinary scheduled trading).
If 1 or 2 fails the rule is closed and the hold-out stays unopened.

## Honest prior
About 7% that the hold-out confirms. The persistence of cell P&L across 2024 and 2025 and the survival of index and crypto cells at minute 5 are the first non-trivial intraday signs in this campaign, but two thirds of the FX gross has already proved to be a boundary artefact, the remaining gross Sharpe (1.3-2.0) was measured with positions tuned to minute-0 marks, hourly seasonality in indices is mostly the known overnight / opening drift that our earlier index tests found small, and 195 days of hold-out can only confirm a net Sharpe of about 1 or more. Market-specific hourly drift can also be plain drift (JPY crosses weakened in 2022-25); the permutation null keeps drift, the hold-out will say whether it persisted.
