Pre-registrations `PREREG_CONFLUENCE_H1.md` (K2, commit 9f42ee4), `PREREG_CONFLUENCE_H1B.md` (K2b, commit 8b36b30), `PREREG_SEASONAL_HOUR.md` (K3, commit 5bcc17a); simulators `confluence_h1.py`, `seasonal_hour.py`; outputs `CONFLUENCE_H1_OUTPUT.txt`, `CONFLUENCE_H1B_OUTPUT.txt`, `SEASONAL_HOUR_DEV_OUTPUT.txt`; diagnostics `confluence_h1_diag.py` / `CONFLUENCE_H1_DIAG_OUTPUT.txt`; ledger families confluence_h1, confluence_h1b, seasonal_hour. Registered bar (K2/K2b): POOL daily t >= 2.4 (K2b: 2.6) with net Sharpe >= 1.0, both years > 0, permutation p <= 0.01, IC t >= 2. All eight models fail the first two; the permutation p (0.001) and the IC pass only because the null (random re-timing of the same churning positions) is even more negative than the models, which shows structure relative to random timing and says nothing about profit. Controls: long-only drift on the 14 non-FX markets earns +1.24 (t +1.79) in the same bars; shuffled-label G1 gives IC 0 and, after the repair, net -2.7 (the churn cost of noise).

## What happened, in order (read this before trusting any number above)
1. **K2 had an execution-realism defect, found from its own by-hour table.** A decision at server hour 23 executes at the first minute of the 00:00 bar, the rollover minute: the quoted spread there is 6 times normal on FX (EURUSD 2.79 bp against 0.43; median over all markets 8.45 bp against 0.9) and the mid is distorted. The models learned to trade that distortion (gross Sharpe +5 to +18, even +12.9 for the shuffled-label control) and paid for it in spread (net -4 to -14). Those eight K2 numbers are therefore not evidence about tradable hours; they are reported unchanged and the defect is documented.
2. **K2b** (re-registered before running, Bonferroni raised to eight trials, t bar 2.6) forced decisions in hours 22, 23 and 0 flat and removed them from training. The gross collapsed to +0.3 / +0.4 for boosting (the GBM has no edge worth its costs) and +3.9 / +1.6 for seasonality, still far below the cost of trading every hour (gross 22 bp/day against 66 bp/day of costs, 8.5 position changes a day per market).
3. **The seasonality gross was mostly a boundary artefact.** Re-marking the very same S1b positions at the mid of minute 5 instead of minute 0 cut the gross Sharpe from 3.2 / 4.4 (2024 / 2025) to 1.3 / 2.0 and FX gross from 9,016 to 3,128 bp (index 625 to 664, crypto 1,891 to 1,204 bp). Rule for every future intraday test in this repo: never mark or execute at the first minute of an hour, and never inside the 23:00-01:00 server window.
4. **K3** was written after that look (so 2024-25 count as development only) to ask whether a SPARSE set of strongly significant cells survives costs: it selects nothing. In the 2022-23 and 2022-24 training windows only 4 and 3 of 924 cells reach |t| >= 4 (placebo 0 and 1), the distribution of cell t-statistics is only slightly wider than the placebo (sd 1.08-1.14 against 1.0), and the cells that do stand out are below their own round-trip cost: XRPUSD at hours 3, 6, 9, 20, 21 (+-10 to +20 bp per hour against 28 bp of cost) and JPY crosses at the 22:00 bar (GBPJPY, CHFJPY, USDJPY, EURJPY: +0.6 to +1.1 bp per hour, t 4 to 5, against 0.3 to 1.0 bp of cost). Hour-of-day effects exist and are smaller than the spread, the picture of a market that is efficient after costs.

## What this means
- The hourly indicator-confluence axis is closed: no model beat the cost wall, and the one place where a persistent pattern shows (JPY crosses in the 22:00 bar, +0.6 to +1.1 bp per hour gross, roughly one pip) leaves at most a few tenths of a bp per day after a round-trip cost of 0.3-1.0 bp, from one to four cells and four years of evidence. For the record, the only cell that would pass K3's rule on the 2022-25 training window (for a 2026 test) is USDJPY at the 22:00 bar (+0.77 bp per hour, cost 0.51 bp, t +4.3): one cell, not a strategy, and the registered rule keeps the hold-out closed. No further variant (thresholds, horizons, hours) is to be run on this panel; that would be tuning on data already seen.
- Two cross-sectional ICs of +0.02 to +0.04 with t of 5-16 are the first statistically significant predictive signal in this campaign, but they were measured on first-minute-to-first-minute labels, which the minute-5 control shows are partly a quote-boundary artefact; this was recomputed on 2026-10-10 (see the addendum below) and the lead is closed.
- With the daily panel (`CONFLUENCE_PANEL_RESULT.md`) the picture is consistent across 29 daily and 42 hourly markets: no interaction of public price features survives out of sample, only drift looks like edge, and costs end whatever small timing information exists. The gold pair remains the only confirmed edge in the repo.
- Remaining ideas that do not use more price-only indicators: event timing (a release calendar is not in the data), positioning / sentiment feeds, and sizing policy (`DP_SIZING_RESULT.md`).

## Addendum (2026-10-10 14:24, local clock): the IC re-measured on minute-5 labels, and what it is worth in basis points
Diagnostic only, not a trial (`confluence_h1_ic_min5.py`, `CONFLUENCE_H1_IC_MIN5_OUTPUT.txt`, `CONFLUENCE_H1_IC_BP_OUTPUT.txt`; the walk-forward models, test 2024-25, decision hours 22/23/0 excluded, adjacent bars only). The first-minute label splits as y = core5 + a(k+1) - a(k+2), where a is the move of the first five minutes of a bar, so the IC can be separated into what a minute-5 execution can earn (core5) and the boundary term it cannot.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 (1h seasonality) | +0.015 | +0.015 | +0.0065 (+2.2) | +0.017 (+5.8) |
| S4 (4h seasonality) | +0.032 | +0.032 | +0.022 (+4.9) | +0.021 (+7.4) |
| G1 (1h boosting) | +0.021 | +0.019 | +0.011 (+3.7) | +0.011 (+5.7) |
| G4 (4h boosting) | +0.021 | +0.026 | +0.020 (+3.8) | +0.006 (+2.9) |
- About half of the 1-hour IC and about two thirds of the 4-hour IC survive at minute 5 (the K2 published figures of +0.03..+0.04 included the rollover rows, which were the artefact of the first run). So a small cross-sectional predictability of about 0.01-0.02 is real, mostly at the 4-hour horizon.
- It is worth far less than its t-statistic suggests. Top-minus-bottom quintile of the prediction, each hour, held 4 hours from the minute-5 mid: S4 +0.81 bp per leg, G4 +0.35 bp per leg; held 1 hour: S1 +0.13, G1 +0.22 bp per leg. The mean one-way cost is 1.7 bp (FX about 0.5, but crypto and oil 4-10). Only crypto reaches a gross of several bp (S4 +5.3 bp per leg, G1 +1.8) and it costs 4-10 bp one way. Every figure is below its cost.
- Verdict: the minute-5 lead is closed. The hourly price-only axis has a statistically visible, economically unusable signal. No further variant is run on this panel.
