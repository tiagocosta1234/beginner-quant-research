## A2 - pass probability as a block bootstrap with a 95% interval (plan A = x1.2 plain; plan B = x1.2 + catch-up to x1.8)
Method: stationary block bootstrap (mean block 5 days) of the guarded daily series of the pair (loss cap 2,000 only, no profit cap, no pass lock), 200 resamples of the history x 2,000 paths. The interval is uncertainty in the day distribution, not path luck. "12M" = last 12 months on MT5 broker real ticks (gold in a strong trend); "5Y" = 2022-26 MT5 broker trades scaled to 4,100/price on MT5 broker bars; edge kept h = every day shifted down by (1-h) x the pool mean.

## A3 - weekend policy (positions closed 10 minutes before every closure > 30 h and not reopened)

Pass probability by policy (plan A, 50/50 mix, edge 1.0; 12M only in brackets): HOLD 32.2 / 50.3 (12M 50.4 / 71.7) | FLAT_ALL 22.1 / 38.0 (39.3 / 62.0) | FLAT_SDB 23.1 / 39.6 | FLAT_XAU5 31.1 / 48.3 (51.8 / 73.9). Closing everything every weekend costs about 10 points of the 30 Nov probability; closing only the 5-minute EA's positions costs about 1 point and halves the exposure of the book (12 oz instead of 24) because the SDB leg is the one that carries the weekend profit. Breach in the history is ~0.5-0.8% either way: the historical weekends are not where the risk shows, the tail is.
