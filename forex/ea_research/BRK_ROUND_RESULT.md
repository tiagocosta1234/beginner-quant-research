0 of 2 pass. A breakout through a fresh round number behaves like a breakout through any other level and like a random entry: Osler's stop-loss cascades at round numbers (1990s interbank order books) are not visible in MT5 broker CFD prices 2022-2026.

**Bug found (amendment B1):** the first run returned -0.27 R (K1) and -0.24 R (K2). The engine let a stop order filled INSIDE a minute be stopped by that minute's low and filled at that minute's open, which can both come before the fill (BTCUSD: a third of trades below -1.05 R, worst -16 R). Fixed (`brk_hours.hold_fill`: the fill minute stops the trade only if it closes beyond the stop). The bug matters only when the stop is within one minute's range of the entry. Check of today's other engines with the same fix (`fillfix_diag.py`): BRK-HOURS and BRK-SQUEEZE identical to 3 decimals (0 of 22,365 NSDQ hour trades change), BRK-FLAG better by 0.001-0.002 R: their conclusions stand. Box-stop rounds are unaffected for the same reason (the minute would have to span the whole box); the card's legs were also checked against real ticks earlier (TICK_AUDIT, ticks slightly worse than the M1 engine).
