# Pre-registration: ICT concepts as exact rules, scanned over every MT5 broker symbol (family ICT) (written 2026-10-12, before the scan was run)

the trader: "(original wording omitted)". The Fibonacci 79% + order-block idea was already tested causally and failed (`FIB_OB_RESULT.md`, `FIB_OB_MIRROR_RESULT.md`). This round formalises the other core ICT building blocks (liquidity sweeps at known highs/lows, fair value gaps, killzones) and tests them on 46 MT5 broker symbols with false-discovery control. 4 variants x 46 symbols = 184 tests.

## Data, clock and execution
MT5 broker M1 bid/ask (`data_mt5`, 2021-12 .. 2026-10-02, swap-free), 46 symbols. All times America/New_York (NY). Bars: M15 built from M1 on the NY clock (bid high = max(bid-open, bid-close) of the minute). Spread floor: ask = bid + max(spread, the symbol's median spread of the real-tick period 2025-10 .. 2026-09). Long enters at the ask / exits at the bid, short the reverse; stops and targets minute by minute (stop before target in the same minute, a gap through a stop exits at the open); a rule is read at the CLOSE of an M15 bar and acted on the next minute, except the fair-value-gap variants whose entry is a resting limit order. Result = R multiple = P&L / initial risk (the stop distance), spread paid. A signal whose stop distance is below 4 spreads is skipped (cost control, fixed here).
## The 4 variants (one trade per symbol and day, per window where stated)
- SWEEP_ASIA (Judas / turtle-soup at the Asian range): Asian range = high/low from 20:00 NY the previous day to 24:00. In the London killzone (M15 bars closing between 02:00 and 05:00 NY): the first bar whose high exceeds the range high but closes back below it -> SHORT (low swept and closed back above -> LONG) at the next minute; stop = that bar's extreme; target = 2R; forced exit 10:00 NY.
- SWEEP_PD (previous-day liquidity raid in the NY AM killzone): the previous trading day's high/low; M15 bars closing between 08:45 and 11:00 NY: same sweep-and-close-back rule, same stop, target 2R, forced exit 13:00 NY.
- FVG_KZ (fair value gap retracement): a 3-bar M15 gap (bullish: low of bar k > high of bar k-2; bearish: high of bar k < low of bar k-2) completed by a bar closing in the London (02:00-05:00) or NY AM (08:30-11:00) killzone, traded in the direction of the displacement with a resting limit at the gap midpoint, valid for the next 8 bars, cancelled if the stop level (the extreme of bar k-2 on the far side) trades first; fill at min(midpoint, open) for longs (max for shorts); target 2R; exit 3 hours after the fill or 16:00 NY. One trade per killzone per day.
- SB (Silver Bullet): the same gap rule but only for gaps completed by a bar closing 10:00-11:00 NY, the limit valid until 11:00, target 2R, forced exit 12:00 NY.
