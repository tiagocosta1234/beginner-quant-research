# When do the +2,000 days happen? (2026-10-13, big_day_anatomy.py)
15 questions fixed in the script docstring before the run; outcome = day whose P&L high reached +2,000 (MT5 broker pool 12.4% of days, FT pool 6.6%); each question tested per pool and pooled (Fisher), Bonferroni 0.05/15 = 0.0033, and the pattern must have the same sign in both pools.
No pattern in: weekday, CPI / NFP / FOMC / any-event day, previous-day gold range, 5-day gold trend, previous day of the system. Only **trailing 10-day system P&L > 0** passes: big days are 13.3% vs 9.7% (MT5 broker) and 8.1% vs 4.4% (FT), pooled p = 0.0032 (borderline). It is the regime effect already seen in IM2 (the system makes its big days when gold trends); it says WHEN the system works, not a new signal, and sizing up on it (throttle T2) lost 14 points in the good regime. The safety version (T1: size down when the trailing 10-day P&L is negative) is the free option already documented in IMPROVE_ROUND_RESULT.md. Nothing live changed.
