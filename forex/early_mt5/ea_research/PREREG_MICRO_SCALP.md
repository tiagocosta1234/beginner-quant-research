## What the diagnostic already says (gs_mechanics.py, GS_MECHANICS_OUTPUT.txt, NOT a trial)
Random entries (Monday-Thursday, 139 days of MT5 broker real ticks 2025-10..2026-09, random direction) with GoldenShot's published exit geometry (stop 20 USD / target 4, trailing from +1.2 following the best price at 0.3-1.2 USD, 0.3 steps, 30-minute cap) win 83% of the time with a mean of -0.4 USD/oz (PF 0.75; strategy 2 geometry 75% and -0.5), whatever the modification latency (0 / 300 / 800 ms). The EA's own tester record is 95.6% wins and +1.7 USD/oz, its live signal 87% wins and PF 1.01. So the geometry alone is NOT the edge: the EA's entry filter selects trades that move +1.2 USD in its favour almost at once, and that selection is what the tester shows (probably inflated by the "pre-entry validation against failure patterns from historical testing" the manual describes, i.e. fitted on the same history) and what the live record only half confirms.

## What we build
A tick-level micro-scalp with the EA's exit geometry fixed in advance: market entry, stop 20 USD, target 4 USD, trailing stop starting at +1.2 USD and following the best price at 0.6 USD in 0.3 steps, 30-minute time cap, one position at a time, entry latency 300 ms (a real broker), costs through bid / ask. Entry windows (server clock): 06:00-14:20, 14:35-15:20, 15:45-21:00, Monday-Thursday only (no Fridays, and the US-data minutes 14:20-14:35 / 15:20-15:45 excluded), at most one candidate every 120 seconds, both directions evaluated.
Data: MT5 broker real-tick days cached in D:\finance\scratch\ticks_gold (139 Monday-Thursday days, 2025-10-02..2026-09-28) and MT5 broker M1 bars for the features. Split in time: DEV = 2025-10-01..2026-03-31, VAL = 2026-04-01..2026-09-30 (no hold-out: only 12 months of real ticks exist).
Features at the entry time, from M1 bars completed before it: returns over the last 1, 3, 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes (USD), the high-low range of the last 5 and 30 minutes, the position of the last close inside the 30-minute range, the 14-bar M1 ATR, EMA(100) - EMA(250) of M1 closes and its 30-minute change, the hour (sin / cos), the spread at the entry tick, the direction.
Trials: S1 = logistic regression on the outcome "net P&L per oz > 0"; S2 = histogram gradient boosting (depth 3, 150 iterations, learning rate 0.05) on the same target; S3 = one hand-written rule in the EA's own words (long when the 5-minute return is positive, the last close is above EMA(100) of M1 and the last 3-minute return is negative (a pullback inside an up-move) and the mirror image for shorts). The model chooses which candidates to take by a score threshold fixed on DEV by the top 5% / 10% / 20% of scores (3 thresholds, reported all, the DEV-best one is the registered one).

## Acceptance (Bonferroni 75, per trial)
On VAL for the registered threshold: >= 150 trades, mean net P&L > +0.5 USD/oz (the EA's tester shows +1.7, its live record about 0; the random control shows -0.4), win rate >= 90%, profit factor >= 1.5, daily-mean t >= 2.5, and the same sign (mean > 0) on DEV. The random control on the same VAL candidates is printed with every result. Cost stress: the result must stay positive with 0.3 USD/oz of extra cost per trade.
Gate for any use: P&L correlation with the pair <= 0.3, clean-guard simulation uplift >= +5 points of P(pass) with <= 1 point of extra breach at 1% risk per trade, tester validation as an EA, forward demo, the trader's go.
Honest prior: about 3% each. Reasons: the lab found no edge in 62 earlier trials, none of them with ticks; a 12-month sample of one regime; micro-structure edges on spread-paying CFDs are usually eaten by costs; the EA's own live record is about break-even. A positive result would be a lead for a forward test, not a finished system.
