# Pre-registration: confluence panel -- does stacking independent signals predict anything? (family K, six trials: a hand-built confluence count, a ridge model and a depth-3 boosting model, each at two horizons)

Written 2026-10-10 13:50 local (local clock), BEFORE any simulation of these rules. the trader (2026-10-10): "(original wording omitted)" (the second PC update moves to tomorrow night). Ledger 214 rows before this family (about 60 earlier trials in 20 families, none accepted); Bonferroni family here: six trials.

## Why this test, and what is new about it
Every earlier test of ours put ONE rule on ONE market or on a small basket, and every "confluence" we tried (round 174 dip-buy agreement, the FVG combo, macro bias + 15-minute pullback, regime overlays on the pair) was a hand-picked AND of two or three conditions on a few markets. Two things were never done: (1) a wide panel (29 MT5 broker, five asset classes) so the sample is large enough to see a small effect, and (2) a model that is allowed to FIND the interactions (that is what a confluence is: a non-additive combination) instead of us guessing which conditions to stack, judged only on years it has never seen. If no confluence exists in daily features across 29 markets and six years, stacking more conditions on one market is not going to create one, and the next lever has to be somewhere else (intraday microstructure, event timing, or the size policy).

## Data (no tuning, no choice after this point)
Daily bars (D1, server clock, from `forex/early_mt5/data/*_D1.parquet`, MT5 broker prices; used only for a go/no-go on information, any survivor is re-run on MT5 broker M1 bars), 2020-01-01 onward only (the trader's rule), weekdays only (crypto weekend bars are dropped, so a Monday return includes the weekend). Data-integrity rule (added during the dry-run, before any P&L): a bar is inconsistent if its open or close lies outside [low, high], high/low >= 3 or low <= 0; for a market only the bars AFTER its last inconsistent bar are used. Only LTCUSD is affected (its 2020 bars have lows of 0.4 against closes of 40; the last bad bar is 2020-11-06), so LTCUSD starts 2020-11-09. Warm-up uses only data from 2020-01-01, so rows are usable from about 2020-11 (the 200-day and 252-day features need that history).
Universe, 29 markets that MT5 broker offers: FX (15) AUDCAD AUDCHF AUDJPY AUDNZD AUDUSD CHFJPY EURGBP EURJPY EURUSD GBPJPY GBPUSD NZDUSD USDCAD USDCHF USDJPY; indices (6) US100.cash US500.cash US30.cash GER40.cash JP225.cash AUS200.cash; metals (2) XAUUSD XAGUSD; oil (2) UKOIL.cash USOIL.cash (from 2021-01); crypto (4) BTCUSD ETHUSD LTCUSD XRPUSD.
Macro context: real yield DFII10 (FRED, `D:\finance\data_macro`), used with a one-weekday lag. The VIX file starts only 2021-11, so it is NOT used (a US500 volatility percentile stands in for it).

## Features (all computed from bars up to and including the close of day t; verified causal by a truncation test in the dry-run)
sigma_t = sqrt(EWMA(alpha 0.06, min 40 obs) of squared daily log returns). Returns: r1 r3 r5 r10 r20 r60 r120 = log(C_t/C_(t-L)) / (sigma_t x sqrt(L)). Distance to the 20/50/200-day mean: (C/SMA_L - 1) / (sigma_t x sqrt(L)); SMA50 over SMA200 cross. Position: IBS = (C-L)/(H-L); place of the close in the 20-day and 55-day high-low range; RSI(2) and RSI(14) (Wilder). Volatility/structure: ATR5/ATR50, percentile of ATR14/C over 252 days, NR7 flag, day range over ATR14, overnight gap (O_t - C_(t-1))/(C_(t-1) x sigma_t), signed up/down-close streak (capped at 6). Candle: body/range, upper wick/range, lower wick/range. Calendar: weekday, month, turn-of-month flag (day of month >= 28 or <= 3). Class code (FX, index, metal, oil, crypto). Context (same for every market on a date): USD proxy returns over 5/20/60 days (mean of -logret of EURUSD GBPUSD AUDUSD NZDUSD and +logret of USDJPY USDCAD USDCHF), US500 return over 5/20 days, US500 volatility percentile, gold 20-day return, real-yield change over 5 and 20 days and its level. Cross-section: percentile rank of r20 and r60 among the markets present that day.

## Label and trading convention
y_h = log(C_(t+h) / C_t) / (sigma_t x sqrt(h)), h in {1, 5}. A signal at the close of day t is traded AT that close and held to the close of day t+1 (the position can change every day). Position weight w = pos x 0.0020 / sigma_t (each market targets 0.2% daily volatility at |pos| = 1), P&L of day t+1 = sum_i w_i x simple return_i,t+1 - sum_i |w_i,t - w_i,t-1| x cost_i. One-way costs in bp of notional, deliberately generous round numbers: FX majors (EURUSD USDJPY GBPUSD USDCHF USDCAD AUDUSD NZDUSD) 0.6, other FX 1.2, indices 2.0, gold 1.2, silver 3.0, oil 4.0, crypto 8.0. No financing (MT5 broker accounts are swap-free). Entering at the close is slightly optimistic (the rollover spread is wide); any survivor is re-costed on MT5 broker M1 bars at 22:00 server time.

## The six trials (fixed now)
C1 (no fitting): six components, each in {-1, 0, +1}: A sign(r120); B sign(SMA50 - SMA200); C +1 if r5 <= -1.0, -1 if r5 >= +1.0, else 0; D +1 if RSI2 < 10, -1 if RSI2 > 90, else 0; E +1 if IBS < 0.2, -1 if IBS > 0.8, else 0; G context: indices and crypto sign(US500 20-day return), gold and silver -sign(real-yield 20-day change), FX pairs quoted in USD sign(-USD proxy 20-day return), USD-base pairs (USDJPY USDCAD USDCHF) sign(+USD proxy 20-day return), oil and FX crosses 0. Score = A+B+C+D+E+G; trade pos = sign(score) when |score| >= 3, else flat. C1a holds one day; C1b keeps the last non-zero signal for 5 bars (the newest signal replaces the old one).
C2 (ridge, alpha 1000, standardised numeric features, NaN filled with the training mean, weekday / month / class as one-hot columns) and C3 (HistGradientBoostingRegressor: max_depth 3, learning_rate 0.03, max_iter 200, min_samples_leaf 300, l2 10, random_state 0, NaN native, class code as an integer) are each fitted to y_1 (trials C2a, C3a) and to y_5 (C2b, C3b) with squared error on labels clipped to +-4, pooled over all markets. Walk-forward: for test year Y in 2023, 2024, 2025 (and 2026 only if the hold-out opens) the model is fitted on rows whose label window ends before Jan 1 of Y (this is the embargo), frozen, and predicts year Y; nothing is tuned and no hyper-parameter is chosen on any test year. Scale: a second model fitted the same way one year earlier predicts year Y-1, and s = the standard deviation of those genuinely out-of-sample predictions; pos = clip(prediction / (2 s), -1, 1).
Diagnostics that are NOT trials: dose-response of C1 (mean y by score), mean daily rank IC of each model, results by asset class, shuffled-label control for C3 (labels permuted within date; must give Sharpe near 0 and IC near 0), permutation importance of the C3 features on 2025, and the truncation test of causality.

## Periods
PRE (C1 only, the unfitted rule): 2021-01-01..2022-12-31. DEV 2023-01-01..2024-12-31 (walk-forward out-of-sample for C2/C3). VAL 2025. POOL = DEV + VAL (2023-2025, three years). HOLD-OUT 2026-01-01..2026-09-30: ONE look, only for a trial that passes 1-4.

## Acceptance (Bonferroni, six trials; a trial must clear ALL of 1-4)
1. POOL 2023-2025 net: daily t >= 2.4 (one-sided p < 0.0083, which is a Sharpe of about 1.4 over three years) AND net Sharpe >= 1.0.
2. Sign consistency: net Sharpe > 0 in each of 2023, 2024 and 2025, and 2025 >= 0.3. For C1 also PRE net Sharpe > 0.
3. Permutation: p <= 0.01 against 1,000 random circular shifts of each market's position series (keeps the autocorrelation of the signal; breaks its timing against returns).
4. Mean daily rank IC (Spearman, prediction vs y_h, across the 29 markets each day) > 0 with t >= 2 (not applicable to C1, which has no continuous prediction).
5. HOLD-OUT 2026 net Sharpe > 0 (one look).
6. Gate for any use: daily correlation with the gold pair <= 0.3; the clean simulation shows uplift in P(pass) with at most 1 point of extra breach; re-cost and re-run on MT5 broker M1 bars; fixed risk and hard stops; AccountGuard-compatible. A daily Sharpe near 1.4 is real money for the live stage, but it CANNOT shorten the test (the pass-speed gate needs a daily Sharpe near 0.3, annualised about 5): this family is for the edge, not the clock, and the report says so.
If 1 fails the trial is closed (no hold-out).

## Honest prior
About 3% that any of the six clears the bar. Daily-horizon equity/FX/metal signals are the most mined data in finance, our own earlier daily tests (trend basket, index daily, NR7, crypto trend) were negative net, and a Sharpe of 1.4 net on a 29-market panel would be a large, surprising finding. The reasons it is still worth running: it is the first test with enough breadth and an honest out-of-sample protocol to say whether an interaction exists, a clean negative redirects the search, and a weak positive in the IC (not the Sharpe) would tell us which feature family deserves an intraday follow-up.
