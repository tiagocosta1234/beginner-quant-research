# Pre-registration: a system that ends the day at +X before -X more than 60% of the time (written 2026-10-13, before any barrier label was computed)
the trader: "um sistema que tenha uma chance de o dia acabar em +2000 superior a 60%". With symmetric barriers (win +X, loss -X) a 60% hit rate equals a mean of +0.20 R per trade before the timeouts, so the target is stated as the pre-registered numbers below, not as a hit rate chosen after the fact. This is the same ML pipeline as ML_TV (raw 48-bar windows + realised variance + spread + relative tick volume + time) with a new LABEL: first passage of a symmetric bracket.
Label: decision at a 15-minute bar close with server hour in 1..14 (so the 8-hour window never touches hours 23 and 0, the rollover artefact); entry at the next bar's ask (long) / bid (short); barrier X = k x (1-hour volatility = 4 x the causal 384-bar std of bar returns) of the entry price, k in {1, 2}; long: win when the BID high reaches entry+X, loss when the BID low reaches entry-X; short: win when the ASK low reaches entry-X, loss when the ASK high reaches entry+X; both inside one 15-minute bar = loss (conservative); window 32 bars, timeout exit at the bid (ask) close, R = pnl/X. R is therefore after the real spread. One long label and one short label per row.
Models: XGBoost regressors as in ML_TV (same hyper-parameters), one per side, trained on window A (2022-01..2023-06, rows on the 4-bar grid), early stopping on B (2023-07..2024-12), tau = quantile (50..95%) of the best predicted R on B maximising the mean R on B with >= 100 trades; VAL (2025) once. Entries on a grid of 8 bars within each market.
Cells: 4 classes (fx, idx, cmd, crypto) x k in {1, 2} = 8. A cell PASSES only if on VAL: >= 100 trades, >= 20 weekly clusters, mean R >= +0.20 (a ~60% hit rate with symmetric barriers), the excess over random timing (same side and market) has cluster-robust one-sided p < 0.05 / 8. Hit rate (wins / (wins + losses)) is reported beside it.
Calibration: placebo worlds 101-103 (shuffled bars) must pass 0 cells each (else <= 1 of 10 by the sequential rule); the real run is read only after that. 2026 stays sealed. VAL 2025 has been seen by other labs: a pass is a screening result that still needs the sealed hold, the MT5 broker environment add-on test and tester ticks.
Expectation written now: the target is an edge of 0.2 R per trade after costs, far above the best z in any family so far (the noise ceiling of the ML lab is IC 0.02-0.03). The most likely outcome is no passing cell; the value is a calibrated answer to the exact question asked.
