The Ninja Turtle Scalper uses the Donchian Channel to find entry signals — the same indicator that made the legendary Turtle Traders famous. Simple in concept, proven in practice, and now built right into MT5.

If you have always wanted to use the Donchian signals as a scalping entry trigger, this is the EA for you. Important: live results can differ from backtests. The strategy uses a very tight trailing stop, which can lead to slippage around news events and fast moves.
